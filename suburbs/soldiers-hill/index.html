What I’m seeing in Soldiers Hill

Single-fronted Victorian cottages with original features are the hottest stock in Ballarat right now. Three or more offers is normal.

Buyers here are price-sensitive around the $600k mark. Pricing just under a round number brings noticeably more enquiry.

Off-street parking via a rear lane adds real value. Make sure it shows up in the photos and the copy.

Common questions

What's the median house price in Soldiers Hill?

The sample median for the last 12 months is $585,000, up 5.6%. Renovated cottages regularly sell between $620,000 and $700,000.

How quickly do Soldiers Hill homes sell?

The suburb average is about 24 days. Well-presented cottages often sell in under two weeks.

Is Soldiers Hill a good place to sell right now?

Yes, for character homes in good condition. Demand from first-home buyers and investors is strong and supply is tight.
