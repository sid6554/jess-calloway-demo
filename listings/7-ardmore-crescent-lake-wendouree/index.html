Red-brick Federation home with a terracotta roof, bay windows, four bedrooms and a deep garden, one block from Wendouree Parade.

This is the kind of house people wait years for. It's solid Federation brick with leadlight bay windows and a terracotta roof, on an 818 m² block one street back from the lake.

The original four bedrooms and formal lounge are intact. A 2019 rear extension added an open kitchen, family room and a second bathroom without touching the street-facing rooms.

Out the back are established fruit trees, a brick workshop and room for a pool if you want one. Ballarat Grammar and the Botanical Gardens are both a short walk away.
